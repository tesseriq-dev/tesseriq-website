Four simple steps from contact list to settled ledger

Get started in minutes

No training needed. Works the way you already think.

1

Build Your Network

Start by adding the people you already work with. Tesseriq helps you organize your driver and load owner network so you can stop depending on memory and call logs.

  • • Add name, mobile number, and vehicle details
  • • Set vehicle type (Open/Closed), wheel count, and capacity
  • • Mark preferred routes and availability
  • • Bulk import from contacts for speed
2

Post & Discover Loads

When a load comes in, post the details once and let Tesseriq surface the best-fit drivers. You can also post available drivers to discover loads that match their routes and capacity.

  • • Enter load type, weight, pickup and dropoff locations
  • • See matching drivers with % compatibility score
  • • Or post available drivers to find matching loads
  • • No more endless phone calls to check availability
3

Create & Track Trips

Once you confirm a match, create the trip and move it forward with clear milestones. Everyone stays aligned on the current status without repeated follow-up calls.

Assigned At Pickup Loading In Transit At Delivery Unloading ✓ Complete
4

Settle Payments

Every trip carries money in and money out. Tesseriq keeps both sides visible so you can settle faster and reduce disputes with clear records.

  • • Set receivable and payable agreements per trip
  • • Log payments with mode (UPI, Bank, Cash)
  • • See outstanding amounts by person
  • • Aging buckets show overdue payments
