Built for the people behind every successful movement

Empowering India's Logistics Backbone

India's trucking economy powers a ₹15+ lakh crore industry, yet the middlemen who coordinate drivers, loads, and payments still rely on phones, WhatsApp threads, and notebooks to keep everything moving. Tesseriq gives them a professional operating system built around how they actually work.

The problem we solve

For many middlemen, the day starts before sunrise with a flood of WhatsApp messages, missed calls, and urgent load requests. One customer needs a truck by noon. Another driver is suddenly unavailable. Every update lives in a different chat or call log.

To close a single booking, they often call driver after driver, flip through a worn notebook for route history, and manually compare rates and availability from memory. If they move fast enough, they win the load. If they don't, the opportunity disappears.

And after the trip, the same fragmented process creates confusion around payments, commitments, and settlements. Disputes happen not because people don't work hard, but because the tools were never built for this job in the first place.
